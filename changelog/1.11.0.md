Sentinel 1.11.0 Argus: delicate scanning digs in binary

• Delicate live scanning now shows its digging: right after the gold line, the results page corrupts into flickering binary for a moment, gold digits on black. Over each result, the digits take its colour (lemon yellow, orange, red, or green when it is clear), cycling until the verdict is in. It is quick and thin, so your search stays readable, and it fades as soon as every result is checked. Fast scanning stays clean.
• Manual link scans now come in two kinds: Fast (threat lists, the full checklist and known scams, in about a second) and Delicate (all that, plus research into the site: its age, certificate, redirects and page content). Pick one on the Link scan page; a delicate scan digs in binary while it works.
• Plans, per week:
  - Free: 10 fast link scans (delicate scans come with Pro).
  - Pro: 200 fast and 40 delicate link scans.
  - Max: 1,000 fast and 100 delicate link scans.
  - Ultimate: unlimited fast and 500 delicate link scans.
  Delicate scans are what Pro, Max and Ultimate called researched scans before, so nobody gets fewer. A pasted email scan counts as a delicate scan.
• On the website, "Try a link" has a model picker and Fast or Delicate (three delicate scans a day without an account), and the example browser digs into its results in binary.
• Binary across the site: chapter headings arrive out of binary as you scroll to them, a tab click digs into its chapter, and cards show a lens of binary around your pointer.
