Sentinel 1.8.2: steadier marks, safer accounts, and a website that reads well in light mode

**Live scanning**
• Marks no longer run off and snap back when you keep scrolling at the end of a page, or scroll an inbox list. Sentinel now knows whether the page can still move, and follows the wheel at full speed only when it can.
• A browser that is slow to say where its links went no longer throws the marks back to the top in the middle of a long scroll.

**Accounts and security**
• Google sign-in only finishes in the browser that started it, so a link to someone else's sign-in cannot put you in their account.
• If someone had signed up with your address before you, and never confirmed it, signing in with Google now takes the account back: their password and sessions are removed.
• Turning on two-factor authentication asks for your password. Accounts that sign in with Google need a recent sign-in to set a password, turn on two-factor or delete the account.
• Community reports only mark a site as dangerous for everyone when they come from confirmed accounts at least a week old, and never for well-known sites.
• Live scanning never opens the private part of a page's address (sign-in and reset links stay unused), and the browser companion treats private windows as private.
• The app's built-in server only answers requests meant for your computer.
• Deleting your account also removes your account and network address from Sentinel's security log.
• A file that is too large or empty no longer uses up one of your weekly scans.

**Website and app**
• Light mode is readable everywhere: no dark boxes, status colours that pass contrast, and a theme switch in the app.
• Privacy, Terms and Refunds are linked from every page, and the pricing page says plainly that paid plans are not on sale and nobody is charged.
• Your usage, history and scan results are lists instead of rows of three tiles. Pages appear without fading in.
• What you typed into the app's forms comes back when you press back (never passwords or keys).
• Errors are plain sentences instead of system codes.

**Browser companion 1.4.1**
• The website's typeface and colours, a light theme, and results as a list. Download it again from the website to update.
