**Sentinel 1.6.4**

**Faster**
- Delicate live scanning now puts marks on screen straight away from the threat lists and checklist, then refines them when research finishes. You no longer wait for research to see a mark.
- Pages are checked sooner after you land on them (350 ms instead of 600 ms).

**More accurate**
- Catches phishing pages hidden inside hijacked website hosting accounts (addresses like `/~account/admin`), which made up most of today's public phishing list. University home pages are left alone.
- Flags pages served from service-record host names (names with an underscore, like `_dc-mx.`), which real websites never use.
- Address rules alone on today's live phishing list: 29.0% to 44.7%, still 0 false alarms on 127 popular sites.

**Cleaner look**
- Section labels, headings and badges are plain sentence-case text instead of spaced-out capitals.
- Removed the "Most popular" badge from the pricing page.
- Tidier spacing in the app.

The app updates itself. Website: https://wyattbombara.github.io/sentinel/
