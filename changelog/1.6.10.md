Sentinel 1.6.10: marks where you expect them on every search engine, and ads get checked too

**Live scanning**
• DuckDuckGo: each result gets one mark, beside its title. Before, a result's sitelinks could get a stray mark of their own.
• When you scroll a result's title off the screen, its leftover sitelinks no longer each get a mark.
• A second link to the same page (like a "Reviews" link) joins its result's mark instead of adding another.
• New: ads are checked. Sponsored results on Bing, Google and DuckDuckGo used to go unmarked, and fake shops and fake customer-service numbers often buy ads.
• Sitelinks that live on another part of the same website (like a support subdomain) join their result.

**Also**
• Fixed a test that failed only when run on a Monday.

Validation: All 157 automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines, and live scanning was checked end to end on a real Windows desktop with DuckDuckGo and Bing.
