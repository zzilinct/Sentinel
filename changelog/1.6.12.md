Sentinel 1.6.12: inbox scams caught, and scan results that are easier to read

**Email**
• Scams in your inbox list are now marked as likely scams, not just "suspicious": for example "Apple Support" or the "IRS" asking to be paid in gift cards or Bitcoin, fake prize and giveaway payments, and "USPS" asking for a redelivery fee (USPS never charges one by email or text).
• Fixed: the sender's name in your inbox list (like "PayPal Support Team") was being ignored, so a scam pretending to be a company was only judged by its subject.
• Fewer false alarms: a receipt for a gift card you bought, or a gift card someone sent you, is no longer treated as a payment demand.
• Tested on a full inbox of everyday mail and scams: every scam was marked and none of the real mail was.

**Scan results**
• A failed check now says what was found ("Listed as phishing by Sentinel threat database") instead of a red cross beside "Not a known scam".
• Each section opens on its concerns, with the checks that passed one click away, so results are much shorter.

Validation: All 166 automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines, and live scanning was checked end to end on a real Windows desktop with DuckDuckGo, Bing, Google and a test inbox.
