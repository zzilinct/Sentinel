Sentinel 1.9.5 Argus: the black hole feeds

• The black hole now consumes the mask: it opens at the centre and grows, eating the mask from the middle out while the metal near its edge is dragged round and in. The new mask still comes out of the hole the way it did.
• Before the hole opens, the ring joining the scope's four diamonds lights up, arc by arc, from each diamond to the next.
• The hole bends the starlight around it further as it grows, and its disc is drawn as a real one is seen: brighter on the side coming toward you, ending short of the shadow.
• It is much lighter on your computer: the disc and its glow are drawn once and reused, nothing is blurred while it plays, and the 3D masks draw half as often while they only drift.
