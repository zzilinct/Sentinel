Sentinel 1.8.9: black lacquer and gold

**Design**
• The masks are real 3D renders, made in Blender from the same drawings as the icons: the scam mask in polished gold, the malware mask in candy-red metal, the virus mask in burnt orange with its spores, and Sentinel's own mask in black lacquer lit with gold.
• Each mask changes colour with its severity. Pick Suspicious, Likely or Confirmed and the mask itself turns yellow, orange or red.
• SENTINEL in cast gold, with the black mask lying across it. "Unmasked" in gold italic above the line the internet wears masks.
• Luxury type, raised soft panels, editorial chapter numbers, Victorian frames and rules, pencil construction sketches that draw themselves, mist and motes of light.
• Every section has its own setting: a specimen under glass for the live demo, medallions for the numbers, a plate for each mask, a catalogue of tricks, display cabinets, a colour reading where the mask turns as you scroll, a technical drawing, a parlour game, price tickets, a vault and a ledger of questions.
• Clicking a tab sweeps you to its chapter; pricing, download, legal and not-found pages share the frames and the masks.
• Works in the light theme, on phones, with reduced motion, and with scripts blocked.
