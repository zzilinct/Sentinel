Sentinel 1.8.9: more reliable live scanning

- Failed scans retry instead of being shown as clean.
- Results are rescanned when their title, address or search context changes.
- Late answers cannot warn about a page after you leave it or stop protection.
- Nested search redirects, Bing ad addresses and user-published Google results are checked more accurately.
- Incomplete research is retried, and live and manual research results remain separate.
- Community reports must meet verification and account-age requirements before they can confirm a threat.
- Companion 1.4.2 improves contextual scans and private-window cache handling.

All 17 new regression tests pass. These changes fix specific error cases; scanning cannot guarantee zero mistakes.
