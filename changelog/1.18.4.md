Sentinel 1.18.4 Argus: fewer false alarms on short names, and companion marks on Yahoo Japan and Brave

Fewer false alarms on honest sites:
• DPD's and Absa's short names are also other groups' initials and letters in other names (absa.org, a police department's dpd address, a university's absa pages). Those are no longer read as the courier or the bank planted in front of another address.

Still caught:
• Parcel-fee and sign-in pages that use those names, such as dpd-redelivery-fee.top, absa-online-verify.xyz or dpd.parcel-fee.com, are still flagged.

Browser companion:
• Search results on Yahoo Japan (search.yahoo.co.jp) now get Sentinel's marks.
• Brave Search changed its page layout, and its results were left unmarked. They are marked again, in front of the title so a long title cannot hide the mark.
• A new install no longer opens the sign-in page when the companion is already signed in by then, for example when the desktop app paired it.
