Sentinel 1.9.0: the app in black lacquer and gold, an opening, and a launch-readiness pass

**An opening**
• The first time you open the site or the app, Sentinel's black mask arrives as a field of glowing gold cells that glitch in gold, orange and red, then a scan line resolves it into the real render, the name rises beneath it and the curtain lifts. Once per visit; a click, a key or a scroll skips it.

**The app**
• The app wears the site's black lacquer and gold, with mist and motes of light behind it.
• Every page has an entrance of its own, also played as parts scroll into view: the overview unveils, protection settles, link scan slides in, the virus scan unfolds, email is drawn out, history cascades, site rules flip down, plans are dealt like cards, security opens like doors, assistants pop forward.
• A gold, orange and red sweep crosses the top of the page when you change pages. Titles rise word by word over a Victorian rule that draws itself, and numbers count up.
• The black mask watches over the overview and leans toward your pointer. Panels catch a light that follows the pointer, sidebar links grow a gold thread, rows and icons lean, and everything you press gives a little.
• Meters fill as they appear, and a gold scan line reads each verdict's mask as it lands.

**Safer and sturdier**
• New passwords are checked against known data breaches (only the first 5 characters of a scrambled form ever leave the server).
• Turning off two-step sign-in needs your password as well as a code, and every place that asks for your password again shares one limit against guessing.
• Daily spending caps on the paid services Sentinel uses, a per-minute limit on scans, and time limits on every outside lookup so a scan can never hang.
• A health check for uptime monitoring, a dated backup each day with a week kept, crash logging, and faster account deletion.
• Two sign-ups for one email at the same moment, a double file drop, and pressing Enter while the AI is replying are all handled properly now.
