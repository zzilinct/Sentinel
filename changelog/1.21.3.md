Sentinel 1.21.3 Argus: a faster first view of the phone app, steadier scrolling after a rest and calmer colour choices

Your colour:
• In the app, the colour choices keep their hover while the page changes colour, as they already did on the home page.

Speed:
• The phone app's first view, the Overview, is now sent as part of the page itself, so it no longer waits for the app's script before it can be drawn. Nothing was removed from it.
• The page that holds your saved name is private and is not stored by the browser or the shared caches.

Live scanning:
• The first scroll after the page has been quiet is picked up sooner. The pixel reader now wakes on a key press or mouse move, not only on pixel movement.

Search results:
• A two-line result title keeps two lines when Sentinel adds its mark. A title that is too long ends in an ellipsis instead of growing a third line.

Phone:
• The confirm-email bar keeps its whole sentence on a 360 px screen, and fits at larger text sizes.

Behind the scenes:
• A flaky check on the home page's held card was fixed at its cause: a pointer at rest now keeps the held card whatever its edge does.
• The wallet look-alike notification is now photographed in Windows' own notification list during testing, so it can be checked by eye.
• Detection is now measured on a fixed sample of phishing addresses, with the rate for addresses on no list reported apart from list hits, so one release can be compared with the next.
