Sentinel 1.19.0 Argus: gold masks, one warning card, and a quieter computer

New look for the masks:
• The 3D masks on the website are rebuilt with cast detail, and they are lit by the same gold studio as Sentinel's pictures, so gold reads as gold and onyx reads as dark instead of grey chrome.
• They are drawn sharp at their shown size and no longer get cut off at the edge of their box while they move.
• A colour change during the black hole no longer leaves a mask stuck half swallowed.

Live scanning marks:
• Every mark, in the Windows app and in the browser companion, is now the threat's own mask in gold on a dark tile, with a corner seal whose shape says how bad it is: a diamond for danger, a triangle for a warning, a circle for caution. You can tell them apart without colour, and in Windows High Contrast.
• A safe link gets only a small tick.
• Screen readers and the hover tip name how serious each mark is.

Windows app warnings:
• Every warning is now one black and gold card with no title bar, sized to its words, with nothing to scroll.
• The live scanning warning and the shield's questions spring out of the corner island, and leave your typing alone until you click into them.
• Long site names now wrap inside the card, on the gold button too, so the whole name can be read.
• Escape and Tab work on every card.

Lighter on your computer:
• The Windows app no longer starts a program every minute to look at what runs at startup. Windows now tells it when those settings change, with a safety look every 30 minutes.

Website:
• Cleaner type across the site and the app: headings are upright, gradient headings and small labels above headings are gone.

Fewer false alarms:
• Absa Bank Kenya (absabank.co.ke) is recognised as Absa's own.
• The Browser checkup no longer calls an add-on from Opera GX's own store an outside add-on.
