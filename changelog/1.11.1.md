Sentinel 1.11.1 Argus: more binary, steady marks, phone numbers in chat

• Delicate scanning digs in much more binary: nearly half the screen corrupts at once, in bigger blocks, and it digs for at least two and a half seconds instead of vanishing the moment the quick verdicts land. It is also lighter to draw than before.
• Live scanning's marks no longer bounce after every scroll. A mark pops in once, the first time its result appears; after that it just stays, whether you scroll away and back, the window moves, or its verdict is refined.
• Chat safety now flags phone numbers. A phone number said in Roblox or Discord is pointed out as suspicious, and one from the area codes consumer agencies warn about for "one ring" and callback scams (268, 284, 473, 649, 809, 829, 849, 876, and 900 pay-per-call numbers) is flagged as a scam. Place and player numbers are left alone.
• Chat safety is easier to find and shows that it works:
  - The first time Roblox or the Discord app comes to the front with chat safety off, Sentinel offers it once.
  - It has its own switch in the tray menu.
  - Live protection shows what it is doing right now ("Watching Discord now") and how many messages it has checked and flagged, as numbers only.
  - If it cannot read a chat, its badge says so instead of claiming to watch.
