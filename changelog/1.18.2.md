Sentinel 1.18.2 Argus: fewer false alarms, and 1.18's features checked for real

Fewer false alarms on honest sites:
• A year in a name (seguridad-vial-2026, sicherheit-2025) is no longer read as a scam's serial number.
• Short names such as web3-io or mp3-de are no longer mistaken for a scam campaign's numbered addresses.
• German words with "sch" (kunstschule) no longer look like random letters.
• Telegraf is no longer read as a misspelled Telegram.
• Government addresses (.gov and .mil) are no longer read as a brand hidden in front of another site.
• A home camera on dynamic DNS with "secure" in its name is no longer treated as a sign-in page.

Fixed in the Android share sheet:
• A message shared to Sentinel could be lost if the web app happened to be starting at that moment. It now always arrives in Text scan.

Fixed in the browser companion:
• Marks now appear on every regional Yahoo and on many more of Google's country sites, as well as on Google, DuckDuckGo and Brave.
• In Firefox, Chrome and Edge, the companion is kept out of private windows until you allow it. Its settings page now tells you when that is the case and how to allow it.
