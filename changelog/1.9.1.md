Sentinel 1.9.1: a sketch that becomes Sentinel

**A new opening, on the site and in the app**
• Sentinel now opens on a blank sheet of warm paper. A pencil sketch of the page draws itself on it: faint construction lines first, then the outlines of the header, panels and buttons, the headings traced in their own type, the text as scribbled lines, and the masks shaded with pencil hatching.
• The sketch is drawn from the real page, so every line sits exactly where its part of the page will be. When it is complete, the paper dissolves, the pencil fades (turning gold over the dark theme) and the page washes from grey into its colours.
• It lasts as long as loading does: under a second on a fast computer, longer while a slow connection catches up, and never more than 8 seconds. Once per visit; a click, a key or a scroll skips it.
