Sentinel 1.6.7: live scanning on Bing, lighter and steadier marks, sharper look-alike detection (released)

The Windows app release is available and the website is updated.

**Live scanning**
• Fixed Bing results getting no marks at all. In Edge, Bing wraps every result in its own redirect link, which Sentinel skipped. Redirects from Bing, Google, DuckDuckGo and Yahoo are now followed to the real result. Ads that can only be followed on the search engine's side are still skipped.
• Much lighter on your computer: Sentinel re-reads a results page only when something can have changed (you scrolled, switched tab, or new results loaded), instead of several times a second.
• Marks that stay on screen while you scroll no longer flicker or pop in again; only new results animate in.
• A mark no longer jumps to the corner when the result it was following disappears.
• A results check that fails (network drop, scanner restarting) is tried again after a few seconds instead of leaving the page unmarked.
• Delicate scanning finishes a whole results page within its time budget. Before, a slow network could keep the last marks waiting for about 30 seconds.

**More accurate**
• Catches look-alike brands made with doubled letters or a dropped letter ("trezoorr", "m3tamsk") and brand names run into other words on free hosting sites ("xfinitymaillog", "kucoinlogenac").
• Fewer false alarms: everyday words close to a brand name (cloud, mobile, email, trust, team, stream, codebase and more) no longer count as disguised brands.
• Address rules alone, on today's live phishing list (300 addresses, no threat lists): 42.7% to 44.3%, still 0 false alarms on 127 popular sites. With the threat lists loaded: fast 96.3% and delicate 99.0% of phishing flagged, 0 false alarms.

Validation: All 144 automated tests passed, including new ones for redirect links, the page-reading rules, the reader script's syntax, batch time budgets and look-alike words.
