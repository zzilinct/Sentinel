Sentinel 1.9.2 Argus: masks that watch you, models you can choose, and screenshots of emails

**Sentinel's models have names**
• Every version of Sentinel is now a model, named for a guardian from myth by its first number: 1.x is Argus (this one), then Cerberus, Talos, Heimdall and Lokapalas.
• Under the update check, and on both link scan tabs, a picker shows the model you run. Open it, choose Argus, and every version from the first to today is listed with its date. Pick one and Sentinel downloads it from GitHub, checks it against its published checksum, and restarts into it. It is kept until you choose the newest again. Versions older than 1.9.2 were made before models could be chosen, so they update themselves back to the newest.
• The newest three models come with their own allowances: Pro gets 18 hours of fast and 3 hours of delicate scanning a week, with 30 scam and 30 virus & malware link scans; Max gets unlimited fast, 18 hours of delicate, and 90 + 90 scans. Ultimate is the same on every model. Once there is an older free model that stays put when chosen, the free plan uses the models before the newest three.

**Email screenshots**
• On the email scan, drop or paste (Ctrl+V) a screenshot of an email. The app reads its text on your computer with Windows' own text recognition, fills in the sender, subject and message for you to check, and scans it. The image is never uploaded or kept.

**Hover, everywhere**
• The big mask on the home page looks at your pointer, its eyes lighting up gold, and looks away when you move off. Every other mask on the site watches too.
• Your pointer is a lamp: panels, cards and buttons are lit from where it is, so their shadows fall away from it. Gold buttons catch a polished highlight under it, and the gold lettering turns its light toward it.
• Links fill with gold from the side you come in, and drain out of the side you leave. Rows and options engrave a gold border from where you entered, and their round icons turn over like coins.
• Sections now arrive by unveiling rather than fading in.
