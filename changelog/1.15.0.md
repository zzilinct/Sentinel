Sentinel 1.15.0 Argus: before you pay, fake installers, your week and one-key checks

New:
• Before you pay: at the checkout of an online shop whose address Sentinel knows was registered only weeks ago, a calm card suggests paying with a credit card or PayPal, not a bank transfer, gift card or crypto, so you can get your money back. Big, known shops never see it.
• Fake installer catcher: Sentinel now reads where each download really came from. An installer named like Zoom, OBS, Discord, Steam, Chrome, Roblox, Minecraft and other well-known apps, but downloaded from somewhere else, is flagged before you run it: "This says it is Zoom, but it came from zoom-download-free.site, not zoom.us. Get Zoom from zoom.us."
• Your week with Sentinel: a new page and Overview card showing what Sentinel caught for you this week, with real counts only, plus the biggest catch in plain words and the last few weeks as bars. In the Windows app you can turn on a weekly notification.
• Check anything on screen: press Windows key + Alt + S, or pick it from the tray, and drag a box around anything: a pop-up, an ad, a message in any app, a QR code, a phone number. Sentinel reads it on your computer and tells you what it found.

Fixed:
• Pressing Sign in, Create account or Send reset link before the page finished loading could show a page of error code. The button now turns on as soon as the page is ready.
