Sentinel 1.14.0 Argus: hover, texts, wallets and your own sites

New:
• Point at any link, anywhere: rest your pointer on a link on any web page (a forum, a social feed, an email) for half a second, and a small Sentinel mask says whether it is safe, before you click. It works in the browser companion, and in the Windows app while live scanning is on.
• Text scan: a new page in the app. Paste a text message, WhatsApp message or DM, or drop a screenshot from your phone. Sentinel reads it on your computer and tells you whether it is a toll, parcel, bank, job, "Hi Mum" or wrong-number scam, with every link checked. It uses one fast link scan.
• Wallet guard: some malware waits for you to copy a crypto wallet address and swaps in the thief's. Sentinel now hears the swap the moment it happens, puts your address back and warns you. It works for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Tron, Litecoin, Monero and bank IBANs. It is on by default in the Windows app.
• Your sites: Sentinel learns the sites you really use, like your credit union, school portal or work login, and warns about fake copies of them that no brand list knows: "This is not your credit union. You usually go to mycu.org." You can add or remove sites in Live protection, and everything stays on your computer.

Fixed:
• When live scanning found a dangerous page, its warning window and notification had not been showing since 1.12.0. The corner mask still warned, but now the full warning shows again.
• The Windows app no longer pauses for seconds at a time while Defense checks your folders or chat safety reads Roblox's log.
• A browser window that was just opened is now always watched by live scanning. Before, it could be missed for a few minutes.
• Roblox chat warnings switch to the in-game layout as soon as you join a game.
• The pricing page now shows fast and delicate link scans separately, as each plan gives them.
