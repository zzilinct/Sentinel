Sentinel 1.18.0 Argus: share from your phone, and safer web chats

New:
• Share to Sentinel from your phone (Android): add Sentinel's web app to your home screen, and it appears in the Share menu of Messages, WhatsApp, Gmail and your browser. A shared link is scanned straight away, a shared message opens Text scan already filled in and checked, and a QR code in a shared picture is read on your phone and checked. Signed out, what you shared waits on your phone until you sign in. On iPhone, web apps cannot appear in the Share menu, so copy and paste instead.
• Masks in web chats: in Discord, WhatsApp Web, Messenger, Telegram Web and X messages in your browser, the companion puts Sentinel's mask beside links in messages, the same fast check as on search results, without opening them. A message from someone else that asks for a code, gift cards, crypto or a "verify your account" step gets a quiet hint underneath. The words are read in the page and never leave your computer, and you can turn the hints off in the companion's settings.
• Chat safety in the Windows app learns new signs too: being asked to pay in gift cards, crypto or at a Bitcoin ATM, and "I sent you a code by mistake, send it back".

Improved in the browser companion:
• Gmail, Outlook and search results opened in a background tab now get their masks as soon as you look at them.
• The toolbar popup says what live scanning found on the page you are on, Off is no longer shown in green, and a failed scan explains why beside the button.
• If your plan cannot use the detection model Sentinel is set to, the popup says live scanning is paused and how to turn it back on, instead of showing On.
• Keyboard focus is visible again, and screen readers can name every mask and jump between the settings sections.

Faster and more accessible website:
• Every page of the website and web app now passes an automated accessibility audit in both themes, with clearer contrast, landmarks, labels and heading order.
• Pages no longer jump while their fonts load, and the home page's animations do less work while you are just reading.
• The screenshot preview in Text scan and Email scan now shows the picture you chose.

Fixed:
• Two made-up scam addresses on the website (in the practice inbox and the home page's scam swirl) turned out to belong to real registered sites. They have been replaced with addresses that belong to no one.
