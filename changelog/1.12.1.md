Sentinel 1.12.1 Argus: chat safety and copy protection now really work

Every 1.12.0 feature was run for real on a Windows machine, and four bugs turned up and are fixed:

• Chat safety now actually reads Roblox and Discord. Its reader stopped the moment it started, so since 1.10.0 it had been reading nothing. It was tested on a Discord-style chat and a Roblox chat box: phone numbers, secrets and free Robux offers are now flagged beside the message. Check my texts (Phone Link) uses the same reader and is fixed too.
• Roblox warnings no longer vanish a second after they appear.
• Stop pasted commands and Check links I copy now act on what you copy. They had been looking at the wrong thing and never stepped in.
• The QR code checker reads grey-on-grey codes too.

Also proven on a real Windows machine:
- Try a link on the website gives real verdicts without sending your link anywhere.
- The QR checker reads real QR pictures, including a Discord sign-in code in a chat screenshot.
- The password alarm stops your Google password on a fake page.
- The tech-support shield notices AnyDesk and offers a way out of a full-screen fake alert.
- The parent lock refuses to switch protection off without the PIN.
