Sentinel 1.6.18: the browser companion works on Bing and Google again

**Companion add-on (for browsers where the app's live scanning is not available)**
• Fixed: the companion put no masks on Bing results at all, and none on Google results either. Both search engines now wrap their result links, and the companion took every wrapped link for the search engine's own.
• It now reads links the same way the desktop app does, including the address Google shows under each result.
• Ads are checked too, on Google and Bing, and ads that go through click trackers are checked by the shop they lead to.
• The companion is now version 1.4.0. If you use it, download it again from the Download page and reload it in your browser.

Validation: All 174 automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines, including a test that runs the companion's link reading on the same links as the desktop app and expects the same answers.
