Sentinel 1.8.1: marks that move with your scroll, names read like words, and a new look for the website

**Live scanning**
• Marks now move the moment your mouse wheel turns, along the same curve as the page, instead of fading out and waiting for the browser to say where its links went. Sentinel learns how far one notch scrolls in your browser.
• Positions read in the middle of a scroll no longer throw the marks off the page until it stops.
• Sentinel now reads what the search engine shows for each result (its title, and the words you searched for) to understand a site without opening it. A site whose name only resembles a brand is cleared when its own result is about itself, and flagged when its result claims that brand.

**Detection**
• Names made of real words are read as words: overdrive.com is no longer mistaken for OneDrive, while onedrlve and 0nedrive are still caught.
• 106 checks, one of them new: a search result that claims a brand its address only resembles.

**Website**
• Light and dark themes, following your system unless you choose one (the switch is in the top bar).
• New fonts chosen for reading (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, Bricolage Grotesque, JetBrains Mono), clearer contrast and a skip link for keyboard users.
• A Refund Policy page, and the Privacy Policy and Terms now say exactly what happens to reports after an account is deleted and what the site keeps in your browser.
• Your email address is no longer put in the web address after you sign up.
• Pressing a button twice no longer sends twice, and server faults show a plain sentence instead of an error code.
• On a phone, the field you are typing in stays above the keyboard, and the back button no longer empties the sign-up form.

**Security**
• Other browser extensions can no longer read your account through the site, or get a sign-in token for it.
• The server itself checks that the Terms were accepted before scanning, not only the page.
