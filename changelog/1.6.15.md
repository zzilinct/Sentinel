Sentinel 1.6.15: catches more of today's phishing

**Detection**
• Measured on a live phishing feed that Sentinel's own lists never saw, using only the address: fast scanning now flags 36% of the feed's addresses (29% before this release), which is 55% of the phishing domains on it, and delicate scanning 73% of the domains. Not one of 127 well-known legitimate sites was flagged by fast scanning.
• New brands protected: Naver, Kakao, Rakuten, Okta, Alibaba and Aliyun, Taobao, Douyin, NetEase, DingTalk, WeChat, Yahoo, Docomo, Mercari, AutoScout24 and Axis Bank.
• A brand's name put in front of an unrelated website (like "naver." or "chase." on someone else's domain) now counts for more. A company's own space on business platforms such as Okta or Zendesk is never counted against it.
• Catches brand names split with a hyphen ("tik-tok", "coin-base"), brand names in a page's address with numbers attached ("icloud2022"), trust words misspelled in the website name ("sopport", "suport", "acount"), and fake "I am not a robot" pages.
• Fixed: delicate scanning called some universities' websites (like stanford.edu) unregistered, because their registry does not answer the usual way.

**Also**
• Automatic updates are now tested on every release: the previous version is installed on a test machine and must update itself, untouched. In the first test it updated and was back in the tray within 40 seconds.

Validation: All 169 automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines.
