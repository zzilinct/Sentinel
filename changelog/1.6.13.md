Sentinel 1.6.13: download protection that never slows Sentinel down

**Downloads**
• Downloaded files are now checked in the background, away from the part of Sentinel that draws live scanning's marks, so a big download can no longer make it stutter.
• Files are checked one at a time, so unpacking hundreds of files into Downloads no longer starts hundreds of checks at once.
• Clicking a "dangerous download" notification now opens your recent downloads, where the Quarantine button is. It used to open the file scanner instead.
• Flagged downloads are listed first and say why they were flagged.
• Tested with installers people download every day (7-Zip, PuTTY, Notepad++, Python): none were flagged, and each was checked in under a fifth of a second.

Validation: All 167 automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines, and download protection, live scanning and inbox checks were tested end to end on a real Windows desktop.
