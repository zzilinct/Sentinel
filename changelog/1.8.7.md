Sentinel 1.8.7: fixes from a full review of the last six updates

**Protection**
• Fixed a gap from 1.8.6: a script that starts hidden, encoded PowerShell after a long list of options is caught again.
• Phishing pages whose forms carry little scripts (a "<" inside the form's own code) are read correctly again, so their fake sign-in forms count.
• Real security mail is left alone: "we will never ask for your password" and "we'll text you a PIN" are not requests for your details.

**Live scanning**
• Turning the wheel over something on the page that does not scroll (a map, a small box) no longer leaves the marks off their results: half a second later Sentinel checks where the page really is and puts them back.

**Download and startup protection**
• Restore is more careful: if a scheduled task cannot be put back (some need administrator), its backup is kept and you are told. If a file comes back under a new name because something else now has its old name, its startup entries are not pointed at that other file.
• When a file cannot be read, Sentinel also checks Defender's own log before acting, so it works on accounts that are not administrators.
• Live scanning that keeps failing to start begins again at normal speed when you switch it back on.

**Reliability**
• Saving settings no longer fails when antivirus or OneDrive is looking at the settings file at that moment.
