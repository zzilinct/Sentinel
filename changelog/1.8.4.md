Sentinel 1.8.4: more phishing caught, and no false alarm on the pages you really visit

**Detection**
• New check: pages hidden behind generated names. Phishing kits unpacked into keyboard-mash folders on hacked websites, throwaway pages on free site builders, and web hosts' temporary addresses are now flagged. Real names that only look odd ("rhythms", "strengths", "Przemyśl") are not.
• A shared Google Docs, Sheets or Forms link is no longer marked yellow just because Google's own words ("docs", "document") are in its address. A page on those platforms that uses login wording is still flagged.
• 107 checks.

**Measured**
• 96.3% of today's phishing addresses flagged from the address alone (up from 95.0%), with lists that had never seen them held out.
• No false alarms on 127 popular websites, and none on 99 real pages deep inside real sites: articles, shops, banks' own help pages, shared files, site builders and sign-in pages. That second set is new, so every future change is measured against the pages people actually open.
