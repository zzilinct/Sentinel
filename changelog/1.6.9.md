Sentinel 1.6.9: smooth marks, trustworthy inbox checks, updates that install themselves (released)

**Live scanning**
• Marks glide with the page while you scroll, and settle exactly on their result when you stop. No more trailing, jumping or teleporting.
• One mark per website, on its title. Sitelinks and AI-answer citations no longer crowd the page, and marks no longer sit in the middle of sentences.
• Start scanning now brings your browser to the front and maximises it (or opens it), and the gold line crosses only the browser, never the desktop or a game.
• New: Auto scanning. Open a browser and fast scanning starts by itself; close every browser and it switches off.
• Google Sites, Docs and Forms pages in results are now checked instead of skipped.

**Email**
• Fixed real mail being flagged: Google account notices, Canva codes and a church's cancelled event no longer get a mask. Wording alone never marks an email; a mask needs real evidence (a fake sender, a payment demand in gift cards or crypto, a dangerous attachment, a bad link).
• One mark per message: a tick when it is clear, a mask when it is a scam. Marks stay inside the message list while you scroll.

**New**
• What to do now: every dangerous result lists plain next steps, like changing a password or calling your card issuer.
• Check links I copy (off by default): copy a link from a text, chat or PDF and Sentinel warns you if it is dangerous.

**Also**
• Updates install on their own when you are not browsing, and Sentinel comes back in the tray.
• Sentinel's taskbar icon is fixed.
• "paypa1"-style swapped letters are now called out as brand misspellings.

Validation: All 154 automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines.
