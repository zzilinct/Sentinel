Sentinel 1.16.0 Argus: is this call a scam, marked emails, warn a friend and a practice inbox

New:
• Is this call a scam? While someone is on the phone, tick what they are asking for (a code sent to your phone, gift cards, remote access, moving money to a "safe account") and who they say they are. Sentinel answers at once in big words, "Hang up now." or "This is probably fine.", with the reason and what to do next. Find it in the web app at Is this call a scam?, or in the Windows app's tray menu. Nothing you tick leaves your device.
• Warning signs marked in the email: the Email scan result now shows the email itself with each warning sign highlighted where it is, such as "Rushes you", "Name is not the address" or "Link goes somewhere else". Tap a mark to see why.
• Warn a friend: after a scan finds a scam (a link, text, email or QR code), one button makes a picture of it to send to family or friends. The link in the picture is written so it cannot be tapped. The picture is made on your device and nothing is uploaded.
• Spot the scam: a practice inbox on the website (linked at the bottom of every page) with twelve made-up messages to sort as scam or real. After each one, the clues are marked right in the message. No account needed, and nothing is stored.
• Did you type anything? If you leave a page Sentinel flagged that had a password or card box, Sentinel asks once whether you typed a password or card number there. "Yes, I did" opens the recovery guide with the right steps ticked. Sentinel never reads what you type.
