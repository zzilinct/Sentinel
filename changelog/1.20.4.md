Sentinel 1.20.4 Argus: a lighter live scan, steadier marks, and a clearer caution

Live scanning:
• Live scanning rests while you read a still page: it uses about a fifth of the processor it did and costs your browser far less. Any key or mouse move wakes it at once.
• Marks on an inbox that repaints without moving now always settle back onto their messages.

Colour:
• Caution on light pages is a soft graphite that never outweighs danger.
• Warning cards show a full-height mask.
• The companion's hover tip shows caution the same way.

Speed:
• The Overview opens faster on a first visit, and the web app shows its main content sooner on phones.

Motion:
• Animation Off now also stops motion already under way.

Blocked pages:
• Continue anyway on a blocked page goes to the address you asked for, and says when your browser may warn once more.

Galaxy:
• The galaxy's hold under a resting mouse now scales with the card's size.
