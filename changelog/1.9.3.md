Sentinel 1.9.3 Argus: masks in real 3D, and a black hole

**Masks in 3D**
• The masks are the Blender models themselves now, lit in real time: sharp at any size, with no visible pixels.
• The black mask at the top of the site turns to face your pointer in three dimensions, and its eyes glow gold while it watches. The other masks watch you too when your pointer comes near.
• The colour reading watches you as you scroll, and turns yellow, orange and red line by line.
• The threat plates' masks stand on their own: they sway slowly, and spin all the way round when you point at one.

**A black hole**
• Choose a severity on a threat plate and the four diamonds of its scope charge with the new colour and beam it to the centre. A black hole opens, bends the starlight round it, and swallows the mask from the outside in. Then it closes, and the new mask grows back from the inside out.
• It also plays when a plate's stages wrap round from confirmed to suspicious. Behind the dark site there is now a field of distant stars.

**Models**
• The five models are on the download page and in the app: Argus is out now, and Cerberus, Talos, Heimdall and Lokapalas are planned. Each medallion turns over to show who the guardian was.
• Your model sits under the app's logo, and the picker and update check are on every scan page.
• The free plan runs models older than the newest five. Pro has slightly smaller allowances on the three newest. Max and Ultimate run every model in full.

**And**
• New touches under the pointer: the logo's mask turns like a coin, the theme switch swings round, section numbers catch the light, and the colour words underline in their colour.
