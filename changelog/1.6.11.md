Sentinel 1.6.11: marks that stay out of your way, and Google results checked again

**Live scanning**
• Sentinel's marks no longer float above everything. They sit just above your browser, so the browser's menus, Google's apps grid, other programs and anything else you open cover them, the way they cover the page.
• Results hidden under something the page opened (like Google's apps grid) lose their marks until it closes.
• Smooth scrolling: browsers only report where links are a few times a second while you scroll, which made marks lurch. Marks now fade out while the page moves and fade back in exactly beside each result when it stops.
• Google results are checked again. Google recently started hiding where its result links go, so Sentinel now reads the address Google shows under each result (and ignores addresses a website writes into its own title).
• Ads that go through click trackers are checked by the shop they lead to, not the tracker.
• Fixed: the search engine's own menu links (its app, its AI chat) no longer get marks.
• The gold corner mask fades out of the way when your pointer comes near it, so the page's own buttons underneath can be seen and clicked.
• Uses less processor time while you scroll.

**Also**
• Fixed: letters outside English (addresses in other alphabets, accented names, email previews) could reach Sentinel's scanner damaged. This matters for catching look-alike addresses.
• A warning about a dangerous page stays on top only while a browser is in front, and no longer floats over games or other programs.

Validation: All TESTCOUNT automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines, and live scanning was checked end to end on a real Windows desktop with DuckDuckGo, Bing and Google, including Edge's menu and Google's apps grid.
