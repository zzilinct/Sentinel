Sentinel 1.19.1 Argus: calmer scans, a corner island, new type, and an honest result

Calmer, quicker scanning:
• The scan screen now shows what Sentinel is really doing at each step, and moves only when a step is done.
• The waiting added for show is gone, so a quick scan shows next to nothing.
• The rings, the spinner and the gold scan line are gone.
• When a scan finds a threat, the real 3D mask rises in with the verdict.

Live scanning's corner island:
• The logo that sat in the corner of the Windows app's live scanning is now a small island that says what Sentinel knows about the page in front of you.
• Rest the pointer on it, or press Win+Alt+I, and it opens into a panel: the site's verdict and why, the links seen on the page, a one-hour pause for a site found clean, and a way into the app.
• Warning cards spring out of its mask.

New typography:
• Sentinel has new type across the website, the Windows app and the browser companion: Newsreader for headings, Geist for text and Geist Mono for addresses.
• Sizes now come from one scale, so pages read the same everywhere.

An honest result page:
• The verdict comes first, with the one reason that matters most, and the details stay closed until you open them.
• A check that could not run takes one line.
• When Sentinel could only check part of a site, the result says "Partly checked" in grey. A green result only appears when it was earned.
