Sentinel 1.13.0 Argus: a new way to move

New animations across the website, the app and the Windows app.

Website:
• Unmask the hero: move your mouse over the top of the home page and a smoky brush peels the page back to show what Sentinel sees underneath: binary, the scam masks and the warnings it would give. On a phone it plays as one slow wipe.
• The gold scan line now opens each chapter as a laser. It draws in wavy over a faint dot grid, straightens and flares, then the chapter appears.
• Big headings rise into place letter by letter, and long paragraphs arrive a word at a time.
• Some chapters now slide up over the one before them as you scroll.
• The stats count up with a gold bar.
• A field of dots behind the hero mask ripples away from your cursor.

Web app:
• Messages now arrive as a dark island at the top. It springs open into a card, morphs from one message to the next, and folds away.
• Cards, tiles and plan cards pop in with a short spring, and your usage numbers count up.
• Changing pages sends a soft ring out from the link you pressed.
• Page titles carry a small gold label over the heading.

Windows app:
• The live-scanning mask in the corner opens into a card with its message and closes back into the mask.
• Chat safety's warnings open out of a mask beside the message, and they no longer overlap each other.
• The tech-support scam shield's window settles in gently.

Also:
• A phone-number warning in Roblox or Discord now gives advice about the number, not about free Robux.

Everything respects Windows' and your browser's reduce-motion setting.
