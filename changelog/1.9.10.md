Sentinel 1.9.10 Argus: 25 more brands guarded

• Sites imitating 25 more of the most impersonated names are caught: Navy Federal, Scotiabank, TD, Desjardins, Monzo, CommBank, Westpac, Schwab, E*TRADE, Disney+, Hulu, Max, Paramount+, Intuit, TurboTax, QuickBooks, Etsy, Xbox, Battle.net, Riot Games, La Poste, Correos, PostNL and Aramex.
• Their fan, news and companion sites are left alone. Names that are ordinary words (Discover, Target, Citizens) and games with big fan communities (Minecraft, Fortnite, Twitch) are left out on purpose, so sites such as minecraft.wiki are never marked as imitations.
• Measured: no change in false alarms on the 111 real sites with brand-like names, 99 real pages and 127 real sites Sentinel is tested against, and 287 of 300 of today's phishing addresses flagged.
