Sentinel 1.9.6 Argus: steadier and lighter

• The threat plates hold still when you switch between Suspicious, Likely and Confirmed: the example below keeps one size, so nothing jumps and your pointer stays on the buttons.
• The black hole no longer bends the mask before it appears: its pull starts at nothing and grows with it. The plates' spin on hover is gone, since it could start the moment your pointer crossed into a plate.
• The ring of the scope flashes for a split second just before the shot, to mark the switch.
• The site loads faster and runs lighter: the 3D masks load once the page is ready (they were holding up the opening until you scrolled), their models are fetched ahead of time, shadows under the 3D masks are no longer recalculated every frame, and the drifting motes run without using the page's script.
• The top of the S in SENTINEL is no longer cut off.
