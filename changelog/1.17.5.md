Sentinel 1.17.5 Argus: every scam wears a mask, and everyday fixes

New on the website:
• Every scam wears a mask: a new chapter on the home page. Dozens of made-up scams (a fake sign-in page, a toll text, a fake virus alert, a crypto doubler, gift cards in a chat, a QR sticker, free Robux) swirl clockwise out of Sentinel's mask. Point at one and it stops to show Sentinel's verdict; click it for a closer look. It works with the keyboard and screen readers, and stays still if you prefer less motion.
• A calm field of drifting letters and digits behind How it works, as if Sentinel is reading a page's code. It brightens gently near your pointer.

Fixed in the Windows app:
• The pause before paying with gift cards stayed on top of everything after you left the page, even over other apps. It now leaves with its page.
• Uninstalling now removes everything: the startup entry, about 200 MB of downloaded updates, and the empty folder.
• If one of Sentinel's windows stopped drawing (a graphics driver hiccup, for example), it stayed blank. It now comes back on its own, and live scanning's marks return too.
• Open in Sentinel on a warning now shows the scan even when you are not signed in.
• Changes made from the tray now show in the app's Live protection page straight away.

Fixed in the web app:
• On a phone, half the pages could not be reached and there was no way to sign out. A More button now lists every page and Sign out.
• History: delete a single entry, or clear it all.
• The download page no longer hides the download button when GitHub is busy.
• A delicate scan of a well-known site, which needs no research, now counts as a fast scan.
• Pressing twice during a scan no longer shows "Scan failed".
• Screen readers now hear sign-in and sign-up messages, errors point to their fields, and the Ctrl K command palette works fully from the keyboard.
• Small headings in the light theme are easier to read.
