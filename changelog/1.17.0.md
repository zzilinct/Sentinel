Sentinel 1.17.0 Argus: the real site, a pause before gift cards, who really sent it, slow scams and right-click scans

New:
• Go to the real site: when Sentinel warns that a page is a fake copy of a brand or of one of your own sites, the warning now offers one button, "Go to the real paypal.com". It always goes to the real address Sentinel knows, never one taken from the fake page. In the Windows app's warning, the browser companion and Link scan.
• A pause before gift cards and crypto: if a page that sells gift cards or sends crypto is open, and something scam-like happened in the last hour (a page Sentinel flagged, a chat warning, a remote-control program, or "Hang up" from the call check), a calm card says that no real company, government office or bank asks to be paid in gift cards or crypto. "I am buying this for myself" closes it. It never blocks anything.
• Who really sent it: paste a whole email with its headers into Email scan, or drop in an .eml file, and Sentinel says "Really sent by paypal.com" or "Not sent by paypal.com: it came from somewhere else", using the checks your mail service already made. Nothing is looked up.
• Slow scams in a whole conversation: paste or screenshot a whole chat into Text scan. Sentinel looks for the steps that romance and investment scams follow over weeks (a wrong-number opener, a move to another app, a friend far away who cannot video call, talk of crypto profits, a sudden need for money) and shows which step it has reached and what usually comes next. It takes two of the steps before it says anything.
• Scan with Sentinel from the right-click menu: in Windows, right-click any file, choose Show more options, then Scan with Sentinel. The file is checked on your computer. You can turn the menu entry off in Live protection.
