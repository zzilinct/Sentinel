Sentinel 1.13.1 Argus: smoother scrolling

The website's scroll motion is rebuilt on GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis.

• Scrolling the website with a mouse wheel is now smooth: the page glides instead of jumping a notch at a time. Phones keep their own scrolling, and the reduce-motion setting turns it off.
• Tabs and links still land exactly on their chapter.
• The scroll motion follows your scroll, so it runs backwards when you scroll back up:
  - Each chapter's laser line and heading play as one sequence.
  - Paragraphs fill in word by word.
  - The stats count up.
  - The hero mask and the chapter ornaments drift at their own depth.
• Two effects from React Bits, rebuilt for Sentinel:
  - A band of light crosses the gold SENTINEL wordmark.
  - The big gold buttons lean toward your pointer as it comes near.
• Scrolling on a computer is lighter than before.
