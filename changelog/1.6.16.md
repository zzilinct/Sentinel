Sentinel 1.6.16: lighter while you read, clearer tray

**Live scanning**
• Uses less of your computer while you read a results page without scrolling: Sentinel checks the page less and less often the longer it stays still, and picks up again the moment you scroll.

**Tray**
• New: an Auto scanning switch in the tray menu.
• The update line says what is happening: checking, downloading (with progress), or that Sentinel is up to date.

**Also**
• Automatic updates were tested end to end: an older version installed on a test machine updated itself to the newest release and came back in the tray within a minute, without anyone touching it.

Validation: All 169 automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines, and live scanning, inbox checks and download protection were tested end to end on a real Windows desktop.
