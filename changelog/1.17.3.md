Sentinel 1.17.3 Argus: polish for the newest features

Improved:
• Scan with Sentinel (Windows right-click menu) now works without signing in. The file is checked on your computer with the same scanner download protection uses, and the answer shows in a small window right away. Nothing about the file leaves your computer. Signed in, it also compares the file with the list of known threats, as before.
• Fewer false alarms:
  - Text scan's slow-scam check no longer flags ordinary chats, such as a friend who moved abroad or a gift card for a birthday.
  - The pause before gift cards and crypto no longer appears on help pages, news articles or pages about scams.
  - Who really sent it no longer says "Not sent by" for honest newsletters and mailing lists. It now says where the email came through, and that this does not prove who sent it.
• The browser companion never shows the gift card pause on a page it is already warning you about, in private windows too.
• The companion's block page appears at once even when Sentinel cannot be reached, and it asks the scanner less often.
• The companion's warnings now fit small and narrow browser windows, scrolling from the top instead of being cut off.
