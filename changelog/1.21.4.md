Sentinel 1.21.4 Argus: the app wears your colour all the way through, and the website holds still while it loads

Your colour:
• The app draws its mark in the title bar on the sign-in pages, and every colour now reaches the text, buttons and warning windows that had stayed cream.
• Switch knobs follow your colour.

The website:
• The greeting and the Check page no longer jump when fonts load.
• At Larger text on a phone, the bottom bar no longer covers lines or answers, and nothing is cut off or overflowing.
• Keyboard focus lands on the answer heading after a quick check.

Search results:
• A sweep of page widths proves Sentinel's marks never make a Bing title taller.

Behind the scenes:
• A new check photographs every app page in four colours, light and dark, on a phone and a computer, and fails if anything is missing, hidden or still gold.
• A new check lists every visual detail of the website and fails when one is missing, hidden, clipped or covered.
