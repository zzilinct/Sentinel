Sentinel 1.9.7 Argus: more scams caught from the address alone

• Unpaid-toll texts are caught: sites using the names of E-ZPass, SunPass, TxTag, EZDriveMA, The Toll Roads, FasTrak and Peach Pass, including names built from a real agency site (ezpassnj-toll).
• Letters swapped for ones that look the same (paypai for paypal) or doubled (robloxx) are treated as disguises, however short the brand.
• Addresses spelled to read like a government site (ssa-gov-statement.com) are flagged.
• Fake browser and software update sites (browser-update-required.net) are flagged as malware.
• Sites named for an "I am not a robot" check (verify-human-captcha.com) are flagged.
• Free Robux and V-Bucks sites are flagged.
• Fake Norton, McAfee, Geek Squad, Best Buy, Ticketmaster, Shein, Cloudflare, LastPass, Bitwarden, Dashlane and NordPass sites are flagged.
• Fewer false alarms: a number that is a word of its own (1password.com, 23andme.com, 1800flowers.com) is no longer read as a disguised letter, and .edu and .gov sites are never treated as borrowing a brand's name.
• Measured: of 80 addresses written the way these scams are, 77 are flagged (up from 57). 434 real sites and 145 real sign-in, account and download pages were checked, with no new false alarms.
