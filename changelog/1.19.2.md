Sentinel 1.19.2 Argus: stronger account protection and an honest Privacy Policy

Stronger protection:
• If someone stole a browser session on an account that signs in with Google, they can no longer use it to take over or delete the account.
• Other browser add-ons can no longer get access to Sentinel's account on your computer.

A more honest Privacy Policy and Terms:
• They now say exactly what is kept, including the one-line history entry for scanned files and pasted emails.
• They now say how long sign-ins last, and how long backups can hold your data after you delete your account.
• Promises of emails we were not actually sending are gone.
