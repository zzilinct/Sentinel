Sentinel 1.17.4 Argus: tested for real, and fixed

Every new feature from 1.16 and 1.17 was tested the way a person uses it: on real websites, with real files, at real screen settings. Here is what that found and fixed.

Fixed:
• Text scan read only the first 8000 characters of a pasted conversation, so a long WhatsApp chat whose scam came near the end was called safe. Conversations of up to 40,000 characters are now read in full, and for longer ones Sentinel reads the start and the newest messages, where a slow scam's last steps are.
• Email scan said "Could not reach Sentinel" for a very large paste. Now the email is trimmed to what Sentinel reads and scanned.
• Email scan now reads Outlook's message headers on their own, and the right-to-left languages (Arabic, Hebrew) show the right way round.
• Is this call a scam? on a phone: the answer now comes into view as soon as you tick a box.
• Scan with Sentinel (right-click) no longer starts a second copy of Sentinel each time. The answer window fits its words, a file under 1 KB shows its real size, and a clean file no longer lists a worrying-sounding detail.
• At 125% display scaling on a small screen, the bottom of Sentinel's window sat under the taskbar. Its windows now always fit the screen.
• The pause before gift cards and crypto no longer appears on articles and help pages about those scams.
• Smaller fixes: a pasted conversation survives going back a page, signing out mid-scan gives a link to sign in, Warn a friend makes one card on a double press, and keyboard users keep their place after reading an email.

Checked and working on real sites: Google, Microsoft, PayPal and Chase sign-in, Amazon, Target and Apple gift cards, Coinbase, Kraken, Binance and a bank's public page are all left alone, and the companion adds only a few hundredths of a second to a page.
