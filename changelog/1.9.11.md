Sentinel 1.9.11 Argus: free pages dressed as a brand

• Free pages named for a brand with a lure after it are caught, such as a Google Sites page called "shopee" followed by a run of made-up words. Pages named in ordinary words (a school's "applewoodelementary", a "paypalfanclub") are left alone.
• Pages on other hosts named for TinyURL or Bitly, posing as a short link you can trust, are caught.
• A bank's name glued to "bank" or "login" in a folder beside a sign-in folder now counts for more.
• Measured on today's public phishing lists: 291 of 300 phishing addresses flagged (97.0%, up from 95.7%), still with no false alarms on the 111 real sites with brand-like names, 99 real pages and 127 real sites Sentinel is tested against.
