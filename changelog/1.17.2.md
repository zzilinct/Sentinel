Sentinel 1.17.2 Argus: more fixes

Fixed:
• On phones and tablets, some of the website never appeared, however far you scrolled: the Virus panel and the four vault lines on the home page, and text on the privacy page. They now appear as you reach them.
• The download page was wider than a phone, which cut off the menu button. It now fits.
• The download page's installer details ran together ("Version 1.17.0109 MB"). Each now sits on its own.
• Email scan: the advice is now written for an email (do not reply, do not open the attachment, do not call the number in it, contact the company the way you normally do), chosen by what the email actually holds, instead of advice meant for web pages.
• Email scan on a phone: the labels on the marked email stay with their words, and the checklist summary reads cleanly.

Improved:
• The browser companion now follows sites that change screens without reloading the page, as most web apps and wallets do, so the pause before gift cards and crypto and the "Did you type anything?" question still work there.
• "Did you type anything?" now also notices card and password boxes inside a payment provider's frame. As before, Sentinel only sees that a box is there, never what is typed.
• Behind the scenes, every page of the website and web app is now checked at four screen sizes, in dark and light, on every update, so layout problems like these are caught before release.
