Sentinel 1.21.5 Argus: your colour reaches the real app window, and marks follow a Page Down

Your colour:
• Your colour now reaches the real app window: the title bar, the side bar, the Check button, the tray and the taskbar.
• The delicate-scan digits follow your colour.

The website:
• The phone bottom bar keeps its icons and names on one line.
• The hero name no longer slides when fonts arrive.
• Blueprint labels stay off the words at Larger text.

Search results and updates:
• Marks follow a Page Down after the page has been quiet.
• If an update's leftover installer is still busy, the downloaded update is cleared again shortly after instead of being left behind.

Behind the scenes:
• A new check photographs the real app window in five colours.
• Detection is now also scored with no list loaded, with a fingerprint of the verdicts and the list snapshot dates, so releases compare.
• A flaky colour check and the honest-sites check were made steady at their causes.
