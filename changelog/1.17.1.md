Sentinel 1.17.1 Argus: fixes and polish

Fixed:
• The gold SENTINEL name at the top of the website could slide off to one side or down the page once you moved the mouse or scrolled. It now stays centred at every window size. The gold flash in the section headings had the same problem and is fixed too.
• The Windows app's question windows (Did you type anything?, the pause before gift cards) were taller than what they say, with an empty band above the buttons. They now fit their words.
• Who really sent it: an email from a big brand that went out through a mailing service is no longer called a forgery when the headers simply have no answer. It is still flagged firmly when the checks fail.

Improved:
• The pause before gift cards and crypto now also recognises a page by its title and, in the browser companion, its headings and form labels, so a wallet's send screen or an exchange's withdraw box is caught even when the web address says nothing. This reading happens on your device, only shortly after a warning, and only "gift card" or "crypto" is ever passed on.
• Polish across the newest features: clearer focus outlines in the practice inbox, the "Go to the real ..." button gets its own row, a tidier Warn a friend picture ("Why it looks like a scam" when Sentinel is not certain), no repeated sentence in Who really sent it, plainer wording in the slow-scam steps, and a dark scrollbar in the Windows app's warning.
