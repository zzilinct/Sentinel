Sentinel 1.8.8: a new look, and fewer false alarms on real sites named like big companies

**Design**
• Luxury editorial type: headlines in Instrument Serif with italic turns of phrase, text in Geist.
• The gold gradient is back on the hero line and on the gold buttons, with a band of light that sweeps across on hover.
• Soft relief: panels and cards stand up from the page, fields and wells sit into it, in dark and light themes. Corners are rounder again, without turning into pills.
• A new scroll entrance: heading words rise from behind their baseline and sections are unveiled from the top down. Nothing fades in.
• The companion add-on and the app's own windows follow the new look.

**Accuracy**
• Real sites with names like big companies are left alone: 110 of 111 (was 106), including netlify, shopify, paypay, twitch, instacart, linktr.ee and zoominfo. A brand name joined to bait words is still flagged.
• A site's front page is not blamed for a listed page elsewhere on the site: that is a note now. Its other pages keep the full warning.
• A site is not compared against scams that copied its own name.
• Measured today: 96.3% of phishing addresses flagged from the address alone, no false alarms on 127 real sites or 99 real deep pages.

**Live scanning**
• Marks keep pace with the page as it scrolls, sit beside each result's title, and the corner mask steps back after 5 seconds.
• On a fresh install the threat lists start downloading after 3 seconds instead of 20.
