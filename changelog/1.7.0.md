Sentinel 1.7.0: everything from the 1.6.11 to 1.6.18 updates, tested in every major browser

This release rounds off a long series of updates. What changed since 1.6.10, in one place:

**Live scanning**
• Marks sit just above your browser, not above everything: menus, Google's apps grid and other programs cover them.
• While a page scrolls, marks fade out and come back exactly beside each result (browsers only report where links are a few times a second, so following them looked choppy).
• Google results are checked again, ads on Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo are checked, and ads through click trackers are checked by the shop they lead to.
• Now tested end to end in Edge, Chrome and Firefox on a real Windows desktop.

**Email and downloads**
• Inbox scams (fake support desks asking for gift cards or crypto, fake USPS fees, prize scams) are marked as likely scams; real mail stays unmarked.
• Downloads are checked in the background, and a dangerous-download warning takes you straight to its Quarantine button.
• Defense only acts on its own with proof. A startup program that only looks suspicious is left alone, and you decide.

**Detection**
• More brands protected (Naver, Rakuten, Okta, Alibaba, Yahoo and others) and new signs of phishing in the address itself, measured on a live phishing feed with no legitimate site flagged.

**Lighter**
• Much less work in the background: no program started every few seconds to see which browsers are open, threat lists updated by their differences, and no hidden window when Sentinel starts with Windows.

**Browser companion (1.4.0)**
• Works on Bing and Google again, checks ads, and treats inbox rows as previews, like the desktop app.

**Also**
• Automatic updates are tested after every release: an older version on a test machine updates itself and comes back in the tray.

Validation: All 174 automated tests passed on GitHub's Windows test machines, and live scanning (Edge, Chrome and Firefox), inbox checks, download protection and automatic updates were tested end to end on a real Windows desktop.
