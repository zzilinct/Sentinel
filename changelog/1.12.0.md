Sentinel 1.12.0 Argus: ten new protections

Out of 500 ideas, these ten were picked and built.

• Tech-support scam shield: when a page flagged as a scam takes over your whole screen, a "Get me out of this page" button closes it. If AnyDesk, TeamViewer, Quick Assist or a similar program starts soon after a flagged page, or soon after it was downloaded, Sentinel asks whether someone on the phone told you to install it, and can end the connection. It also warns while one of these programs runs and your bank is open.
• QR code checker: drop, paste or hold up a picture of a QR code on the Link scan page and see where it really leads, read on your device. Discord, WhatsApp, Telegram, Steam and Signal sign-in codes are called out for what they are: a way for a stranger to sign into your account. QR codes in email screenshots are read too.
• Password alarm in the browser companion: protect your Google, Microsoft, Apple, PayPal, Amazon or bank password, and if you ever type it on another site, the companion stops the sign-in before it is sent, even on a brand-new phishing page.
• "I think I've been scammed": a new page that turns what happened (card details, money sent, gift cards, crypto, a password, someone on your computer) into one ordered checklist with the right places to report it. It is linked from every red warning.
• Scam texts in Phone Link: with "Check my texts" on, texts you read in Windows Phone Link get a warning beside toll, parcel, bank, job, "Hi Mum" and wrong-number scams, all checked on your computer.
• Stop pasted commands: fake "I am not a robot" pages that copy a command and tell you to paste it into Windows are stopped. The command is swapped for a harmless line, and you can put it back if you meant it.
• Browser checkup: one click lists every add-on, every site allowed to send you notifications, and the search engine and start pages in each of your browsers, and points out the ones scammers plant. Sentinel changes nothing; you remove what you choose.
• Exposure alerts: if a site you visited in the last two weeks is later listed as phishing or malware, Sentinel tells you and says what to change. Off until you turn it on.
• Try a link on the website now gives a real verdict, worked out in your own browser, so the link never leaves your device.
• Parent lock: set a PIN so chat safety, live scanning and the rest of Sentinel's protection cannot be switched off, or Sentinel quit, without it. Parents can see when someone tried.
