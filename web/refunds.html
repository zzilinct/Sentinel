Refunds, in plain words

Part of the Terms and Conditions.

Last updated 30 September 2026 · pre-launch draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer

1. Nobody is charged today

Paid plans are not on sale yet. Switching between Free, Pro, Max and Ultimate costs nothing while Sentinel is pre-launch, and Sentinel does not ask for or store any card or bank details. There is nothing to refund.

2. If you were charged anyway

If any payment was taken from you in Sentinel’s name, it did not come from us. Write to support@usesentinel.technology and tell your bank: it may be a scam using our name, and we want to know about it.

3. When paid plans go on sale

Before anyone can buy a plan, this page will set out the price, billing period, how to cancel, and when you can get your money back. It will be shown at the point of purchase, and you will be asked to accept it. Nothing in it will take away a right to a refund or to cancel that the law where you live gives you.
