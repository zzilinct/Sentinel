The masks

Three masks.
One glance.

Every threat gets its own face, and every face gets a colour. Yellow means look closer. Orange means it’s probably dangerous. Red means Sentinel has proof.

Scam

Sites built to take your money, passwords or wallet.

  • Look-alike and misspelled brand domains
  • Fake login, parcel-fee and prize pages
  • Crypto drainers asking for recovery phrases

Virus

Files that infect your computer the moment they run.

  • Programs disguised as PDFs and photos
  • Known malicious files, matched by fingerprint
  • Archives smuggling executables

Malware

Pages and documents that attack from the inside.

  • Fake browser updates and “paste this command” lures
  • Hidden cryptominers and invisible frames
  • Macro documents and download-and-run scripts

Questions

Good to know

What’s the difference between yellow, orange and red?
Yellow means a few warning signs — worth a second look. Orange means many strong signs at once, so it’s probably dangerous. Red is only ever shown with evidence: the site is on a live threat list, matches a known scam kit while impersonating a brand, or does something unmistakably hostile.
Do I need to download anything?
No. The web app works in any browser for scanning links and files. Live protection — masks on search results, emails and downloads — runs through the Sentinel app, which you download once.
What does “research” mean on Pro and Max?
Sentinel visits the site from its own servers and gathers facts the address alone can’t reveal: when the domain was registered, its certificate, where it redirects, whether its forms send passwords elsewhere, and whether its scripts try to mine crypto or push downloads.
How are live scanning hours counted?
Only minutes in which Sentinel actually checks something for you count, and each minute counts once however many results it checks. Hours reset every Monday.
Does Sentinel need an AI account?
No. Every verdict comes from Sentinel’s own threat intelligence, checklist and research. Connecting ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek is an optional extra for asking questions about a link.
