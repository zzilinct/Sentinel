Sentinel is a warning, not a guarantee

Sentinel flags what it can detect. New scam sites appear constantly and no checker catches all of them, so an unmarked link is not a promise of safety. In the other direction, a yellow or orange mask means "look closer", not "this is criminal". Use your own judgement before entering passwords or payment details anywhere.

Your account
