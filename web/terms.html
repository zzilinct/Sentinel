The fine print

Plain-language terms for using Sentinel during its beta.

Last updated 16 September 2026 · beta

What Sentinel is

Sentinel is a warning system. It tells you how risky a link, email or file looks and why. It reduces risk; it cannot remove it. A site without a mask is not guaranteed safe, and a mask is Sentinel’s assessment, not a legal finding about anyone.

Your account

  • Keep your sign-in details private and turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Don’t use Sentinel to probe, attack or overload other people’s systems, or to scan at volumes beyond your plan by creating extra accounts.
  • Only report sites you genuinely believe are harmful. False reports can get an account suspended.

Plans and allowances

Each plan includes a weekly allowance of scans and live-scanning hours, reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Unused allowance does not roll over. Scans that fail because of a problem on our side are not counted.

AI assistants

Connecting ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek is optional. Messages you send go to that provider under their terms and are billed to your key.

Liability

Sentinel is provided “as is” during beta. To the extent the law allows, Sentinel isn’t liable for losses from threats it doesn’t detect or from acting on its warnings.
