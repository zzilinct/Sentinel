Privacy

What Sentinel sees, and what it doesn't

Plain English first, because the short version is the real version.

What Sentinel sends to its servers

  • The web addresses of search results on a results page, so they can be scored.
  • The address of a page you navigate to, so it can be checked before it loads.
  • Your account email, your name, and a hash of your password.
  • Sites you report, and sites you mark as trusted.

What it never sends

  • The contents of any page. Sentinel reads addresses, not pages.
  • Anything you type: no form fields, no passwords, no messages.
  • Pages behind a login, beyond their address.
  • Your history as a marketing profile. Scan results are cached by domain, not by person.

The AI workspace

Sentinel never asks for your ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek password. Each tab connects through that provider's official API using a key you create inside your own account. Keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage and are decrypted only to make a request you asked for. The messages in a tab go to that provider and are not retained by Sentinel once the reply comes back. Revoke the key in your provider account and the connection stops working immediately.

Third parties

Where a Safe Browsing key is configured, addresses are also checked against Google Safe Browsing. Google sign-in, if you choose it, shares your email, name and profile picture with Sentinel and nothing more. Sentinel does not sell data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Ask at privacy@sentinelscamscan.com and your account, sessions, stored provider keys, trusted sites and reports are deleted. Scan results cached by domain contain nothing that identifies you and are discarded on a rolling basis.
