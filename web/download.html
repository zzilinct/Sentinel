Setup

Protected in minutes

01

Install Sentinel

Run the installer. Sentinel opens, adds its mask to your system tray and starts protecting straight away. There is nothing to configure.

02

No sign-up needed

The app gives your computer its own account the first time it starts, so download protection and defense are on from the first minute. You can sign in with your own account later if you want your history on other devices.

03

Press Start scanning

One button in Live protection, or Scan with beside the browser you use. A gold line crosses your screen, and when the tint fades Sentinel is ready. There is no add-on to install.

04

Browse normally

A gold mask in the corner of your browser shows Sentinel is watching. Search, and a mask appears beside every result. It only works while the browser is in front of you, and private windows leave no trace in Sentinel.

Not on Windows yet?
