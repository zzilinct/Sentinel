Release notes

Sentinel 1.7.0

1.7.0 brings together the 1.6.11 to 1.6.18 updates: marks that stay out of your way and fade while you scroll, Google results and ads checked, inbox scams caught, downloads checked in the background, much less work in the background, and live scanning tested in Edge, Chrome and Firefox. 1.6.18:the browser companion puts masks on Bing and Google results again, and checks ads. If you use it, download it again below. 1.6.17:Sentinel does far less while nothing is happening: no more starting a program every few seconds to see which browsers are open, threat lists updated by their differences, and no hidden window when it starts with Windows. 1.6.16:live scanning uses less of your computer while you read a page, and the tray gains an Auto scanning switch and a clearer update line. 1.6.15:more brands protected (including Naver, Rakuten, Okta, Alibaba and Yahoo), and new signs of phishing in the address itself, measured on a live phishing feed with no legitimate site flagged. 1.6.14:Sentinel no longer ends or removes a startup program on suspicion alone; you get a notification and decide. Known malware is still stopped at once. 1.6.13:downloaded files are checked in the background so live scanning never stutters, and a warning about a download takes you straight to its Quarantine button. 1.6.12:scams in your inbox list, like fake support desks asking for gift cards or a fake USPS fee, are marked as likely scams, and real mail stays unmarked. Scan results lead with what was found. 1.6.11:live scanning's marks sit just above your browser instead of above everything, so menus, Google's apps grid and other programs cover them. They fade out while you scroll and come back exactly beside each result, Google results are checked again, and ads are checked by the shop they lead to. 1.6.10: sponsored results on Bing, Google and DuckDuckGo are now checked, since fake shops and fake customer-service numbers often buy ads. DuckDuckGo results get one mark each, beside the title. 1.6.9: marks glide with the page as you scroll, one per website. Start scanning brings your browser forward and the gold line crosses only the browser. Real mail from companies like Google and Canva is no longer flagged for its wording. New: auto scanning, plain “what to do now” steps on dangerous results, and an optional check of links you copy. Updates now install themselves while you are not browsing.

Setup

Protected in minutes

01

Install Sentinel

Run the installer. Sentinel opens, adds its mask to your system tray and starts protecting straight away. There is nothing to configure.

02

No sign-up needed

The app gives your computer its own account the first time it starts, so it protects you without signing in. Download protection and Defense come with Pro, Max and Ultimate. You can sign in with your own account later if you want your history on other devices.

03

Press Start scanning

One button in Live protection, or Scan with beside the browser you use. A gold line crosses your screen, and when the tint fades Sentinel is ready. There is no add-on to install.

04

Browse normally

A gold mask in the corner of your browser shows Sentinel is watching. Search, and a mask appears beside every result. It only works while the browser is in front of you, and private windows leave no trace in Sentinel.
