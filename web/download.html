Installation

Four steps, about two minutes

Sentinel is in review for the Chrome Web Store. Until it is listed, you install it the same way developers do — nothing is hidden and you can read every line of the extension in the folder you unzip.

Unzip the download

Right-click sentinel-chrome-latest.zip and extract it to a folder you will keep, such as Documents. Deleting the folder later removes the extension.

Open your extensions page

Paste chrome://extensions into the address bar (Edge: edge://extensions, Brave: brave://extensions) and press Enter.

Turn on Developer mode

Toggle Developer mode in the top-right corner, then click Load unpacked and choose the folder you just unzipped.

Sign in once

Click the Sentinel mask in your toolbar and sign in. From then on it runs in the background whenever your browser is open.

Pin it where you can see it

Click the puzzle-piece icon in your toolbar and pin Sentinel. The mask shows the rating for whatever page you are on, and turns yellow, orange or red as the risk rises.

Running your own instance?

If you host Sentinel yourself, open the extension's options page and point the Sentinel server field at your own origin. To let a locally loaded build pair with this site, run this in the browser console on this page, using the ID from your extensions page:

localStorage.setItem('sentinel.extensionId', 'your-extension-id')
