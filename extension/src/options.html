Password alarm

Pick the accounts that matter most. The next time you sign in to one on its own site, Sentinel remembers a scrambled form of the password (never the password itself, and it never leaves this computer). Type that password on any other site and Sentinel empties the box and stops you. It needs you to type the password once: an account you only open with a passkey, or never sign out of, waits until you do.
